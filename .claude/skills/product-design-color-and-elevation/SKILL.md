# Product design colour and elevation

This skill owns how much colour and elevation a product interface uses, and what for.
Which token or layer a rule goes in belongs to `writing-css`.

**[PDC-1]** Strong colour is rare. The interface is neutral, and each strong colour has
one narrow job, like marking the primary action, a destructive action or a status.

**[PDC-2]** Elevation is subtle, has a purpose and is rare. Surfaces separate through
small tonal shifts and hairlines. Save strong contrast for something that needs attention
now, like a bar that appears when there's a selection to act on.

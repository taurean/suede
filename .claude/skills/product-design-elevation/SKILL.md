# Product design elevation

This skill owns when a surface in a product interface is raised, and how far. Which token
or layer a rule goes in belongs to `writing-css`.

**[PDE-1]** Elevation is subtle, has a purpose and is rare. Its purpose is to communicate
relationship and focus, and it isn't used for emphasis. A popover communicates that it's
related to the context it came from. A modal captures attention during a different flow.
Otherwise, surfaces separate through small tonal shifts and hairlines.
